About Us

Planning for the future can be overwhelming. From figuring out where you want to live to deciding on the right university, there are countless factors to consider, especially when it comes to finances. That's why we created this web app to simplify the process and empower you to make informed decisions about your future.

We understand that cost of living and education expenses play a crucial role in shaping your life choices. Our app collects and aggregates comprehensive data on these key areas, providing you with a centralized platform to explore different possibilities. Whether you're considering a move to a new city or researching universities, we provide the data you need to compare costs and make informed decisions.

How it Works:

Our app gathers and presents data related to:

  • Cost of Living:

    We compile information about housing, transportation, groceries, utilities, and other essential expenses for various locations, giving you a clear picture of what it costs to live in different areas.

  • University Data:

    We collect information on tuition fees, living expenses, financial aid options, and other relevant details for a wide range of universities, helping you assess the financial implications of pursuing higher education.

  • Scholarships:

    We gather information on available scholarships, grants, and other financial aid opportunities that can help offset the cost of education. This includes both merit-based and need-based scholarships.

  • State Equality Scoring:

    We provide data and scoring related to state-level equality measures, allowing you to consider factors such as LGBTQ+ rights, gender equality, and racial equality when making decisions about where you want to live or study.

Saving Your Research:

To ensure you can easily access and manage your research, we've integrated with Google Sheets. By logging in with your Google account, you can save the data you find directly to a dedicated spreadsheet. After saving, the app will open the Google Sheet for you, allowing you to:

  • Organize your findings:

    Keep all your research in one easily accessible place.

  • Compare different options:

    Create tables and charts to visualize the data and compare different locations or universities side-by-side.

  • Share your research:

    Easily share your findings with family, advisors, or anyone else involved in your planning process.

Your Privacy Matters:

We are committed to protecting your privacy. We require Google account login solely for the purpose of saving your data to your own Google Sheets. We only collect the data that is explicitly defined in our privacy policy. We never access or store any other information from your Google account. We encourage you to review our Privacy Policy for complete details on how we handle your data.

We believe that everyone deserves the tools and information they need to plan for a successful future. We hope our app empowers you to make informed decisions and achieve your goals. If you have any questions or feedback, please don't hesitate to contact us.
